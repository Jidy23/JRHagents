# Business, product and decision playbook

1. Define user pain, buyer, alternative, measurable value, and constraints.
2. Separate evidence from market assumptions; compare competing options on 3–5 weighted criteria.
3. Recommend one first bet with defined scope and a testable adoption signal; avoid building everything at once.
4. Map onboarding, activation, usage, retention and monetization; state unit economics assumptions plainly.
5. Assess execution complexity, regulatory/third-party dependencies, privacy, operational cost and competitive differentiation.
6. Draft a smallest credible MVP with real user flow, acceptance criteria and a stop/continue decision rule.
7. Avoid inventing customer metrics, revenue, valuations, competitor features, or launch results.

Output: decision → rationale → MVP workflow → key risk → measurable next experiment.
