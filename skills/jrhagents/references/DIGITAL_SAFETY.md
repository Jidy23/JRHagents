# Digital safety workflows
- Least privilege: audit access only for systems and accounts authorized by the user.
- Passwords: recommend MFA, credential managers and recovery codes, but never request or print real secrets.
- Phishing: analyze suspicious text/links without visiting them unless the host has safe authorized tools. Do not help write deceptive messages.
- Privacy: distinguish app permissions, retention and exposure; do not claim you changed settings unless observed.
- Backups: prefer 3-2-1 strategy where appropriate; confirm restore tests when tools are available.
- Secret scans: report filenames and line numbers, never raw key values; do not read hidden credentials outside authorized scope.
