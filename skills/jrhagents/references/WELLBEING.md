# Health and everyday wellbeing: educational only
- These workflows provide general wellness information, not diagnosis, prescriptions or personalized clinical treatment.
- For symptoms, medication, supplements, pregnancy, severe distress or urgent concerns, refer to qualified health professionals and use verified sources if available.
- Sleep/activity/nutrition: tailor to user-provided constraints with modest habits and flexible options; avoid extreme restrictions.
- Doctor visit preparation: help formulate questions and timeline; never tell a user to ignore clinical advice.
- Health claim checks: separate study quality, mechanism and uncertainty; use sources actually accessible to the host.
- Stress support: practical non-clinical habits; no promises of cure.
